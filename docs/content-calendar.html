Your morning

Approval routine

  1. 6:00 AM: package buildsThe videos, three caption options per post and the day's event tags are ready in the dashboard.
  2. Review (about 2 minutes)Watch the four videos, pick Hook, Data or Question, and tick any extra tags.
  3. Tap Approve allEach post goes out at 9, 11, 1 and 6. If you're late, it posts right away (up to 4 hours after its slot).
  4. Event daysThe banner at the top of the dashboard lists bonus-post ideas. Make those by hand, or ask for a custom render.
Sources for event dates: cwsomaha.com, gocreighton.com, huskers.com, the FIVB, CHI Health Center, holidaylightsfestival.org and Berkshire meeting guides. There is no Offutt air show in 2027; it's every other year, and the next one is in 2028.
